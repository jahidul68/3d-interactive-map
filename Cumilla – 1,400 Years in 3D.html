A 3D timelapse from the forested Lalmai–Mainamati hills of the 7th century to today's city of nearly half a million people. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and use the timeline to jump to any year.

About this timelapse

The landscape is stylised. The Gomti, the Lalmai–Mainamati ridge, Dharmasagar and the landmarks are in roughly the right places relative to each other, but distances are compressed and heights exaggerated. Individual buildings are generated, not surveyed.

Population: the 2011 (339,133) and 2022 (440,233) figures are census counts for the city corporation. All earlier figures are rough illustrative estimates for the settlement or town of the day. The building count follows that curve.

Dates follow the sources below. Where they disagree, the page says so. The municipality is dated 1864 by Britannica and 1890 by Wikipedia, and the Shah Shuja Mosque's builder and date are disputed. Years marked "c." are approximate.
